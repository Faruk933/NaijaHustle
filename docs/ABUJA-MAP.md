# Abuja Map Plan

## Geographic foundation

The first world is Abuja, using real-world geography as the foundation for navigation and district identity.

Initial districts:

- Central Area / CBD
- Wuse 1 & 2
- Garki 1 & 2
- Maitama
- Asokoro
- Jabi
- Utako
- Gwarinpa
- Kubwa
- Lugbe
- Airport Road
- Life Camp
- Karu

## Map layers

### 1. Geography

Roads, districts, major routes, waterways, and recognizable public geography.

### 2. Landmarks

Important public places can be represented where appropriate, while avoiding an assumption that every real building can be reproduced commercially without checking rights or licensing.

### 3. Gameplay layer

Game-owned:

- Shops
- Restaurants
- Offices
- Job locations
- Apartments
- Businesses
- NPC homes
- Missions
- Events
- Fast-travel points

## First playable slice

The first detailed slice should connect Central Area, Wuse, Garki, and one nearby residential district.

It should support:

- Walking
- Transport
- Finding work
- Buying food
- Visiting a shop
- Renting a basic room
- Discovering opportunities

## Accuracy principle

The goal is recognizable Abuja geography, not an expensive photorealistic reconstruction. Map data, tile providers, imagery, and landmark assets must be checked for commercial licensing before launch.
